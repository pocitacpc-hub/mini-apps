1 · Úvod

Co je NEPTUN

⏱ cca 1 minuta

NEPTUN is our application for individual electricity and gas pricing.

We use it when a simple price list is not enough.

We need to calculate the price for a specific customer. The price depends on the customer’s consumption, delivery period, market situation, and other business parameters.

The typical process starts with the customer and their delivery points.

Then we work with the consumption profile, select a product, and prepare the price and the customer offer.

In NEPTUN, we can also see the customer’s plan and individual purchases.

2 · Zákazník

Zákazník a odběrná místa

⏱ cca 1 minuta
V aplikaci otevřít zákazníka / oceňovaný subjekt a ukázat OM.

Here we can see the customer.

Under the customer, we have individual delivery points.

For each delivery point, we have the data we need for pricing.

For example, we have the commodity, metering type, distribution tariff, TDD, and other parameters.

We don't need to enter this data in NEPTUN.

We get this data from other systems.

This can be a billing system, CRM, SAP, or another customer system.

3 · Spotřeba

Spotřeba a diagram

⏱ cca 1 minuta
Kliknout na diagram / spotřebu.

The customer’s consumption profile is very important for individual pricing.

For electricity, we work with a detailed consumption profile.

It can have 15-minute granularity.

For gas, we usually use daily data.

NEPTUN can work with historical consumption data or predict future consumption.

The consumption profile has an impact on the final price.

Two customers can have the same yearly consumption.

One customer can use more energy when prices are high.

Another customer can use more energy when prices are low.

So their final prices can be different.

4 · Produkt

Požadavek a produkt

⏱ cca 1 minuta
Přejít na požadavek a ukázat produkt.

Next, we create a pricing request for the customer.

Here we define, for example, the delivery period, commodity, and product.

The product setup is flexible.

We can set it for each energy supplier.

For example, we can have a fixed-price product, a spot product, gradual purchasing, or a combination of gradual purchasing and spot.

We can also work with different product parameters, margins, risk premiums, and approval workflows.

Each energy supplier can have different products and pricing rules.

5 · Ocenění

Samotné ocenění

⏱ cca 1,5 minuty
Ukázat výsledek ocenění / přehled ceny.

During pricing, we compare the customer’s consumption profile with the market price curve.

NEPTUN checks how much energy the customer uses over time.

It also uses the market price for each time period.

From this, we get the basic quotation price.

Then we can add other cost components. For example, we add imbalance costs, risk costs and other surcharges.

Finally, we add the sales margin.

The result is the final sales price for the customer.

6 · Nabídka

Nabídka a akceptace

⏱ cca 45 sekund
Pokud je vhodný příklad, ukázat nabídku nebo akceptaci.

Then we can create the customer offer.

The offer can be a Word or PDF document.

We use prepared templates.

The templates can be different for different products or commodities.

The salesperson can then accept the price.

Before acceptance, the salesperson can also change the sales margin or the final sales price.

7 · Nákupní obálka

Nákupní obálka v NEPTUNu

⏱ cca 1 minuta
Ještě zůstat v NEPTUNu a ukázat nákupní obálku.

After acceptance, a purchasing envelope can be created.

The purchasing envelope connects the customer’s plan with individual purchases.

In NEPTUN, we can see the plan, the purchased volume, and all individual purchase transactions in one place.

The purchasing envelope can work with prices and also with coefficients.

This is important for gradual purchasing.

For gradual purchasing, we can use different coefficients.

The customer can then buy energy step by step.

The timing depends on the customer’s strategy and the market situation.

8 · Nákupní portál

Přechod do Nákupního portálu

⏱ cca 30 sekund
Přepnout do webového Nákupního portálu.

The end customer can also use this part directly.

For this, we use the Purchasing Portal.

It is a simple web self-service portal connected to NEPTUN.

The customer does not have access to the full NEPTUN application.

They only see their own data and selected functions.

9 · Tranže

Nákup tranže

⏱ cca 1,5 minuty
Ukázat obálku a ideálně dialog pro nákup.

Here the customer can see their purchasing envelope and make another purchase.

For example, the customer can buy a profile tranche.

In this case, the customer buys a part of the planned consumption profile.

Another option is a baseload tranche.

In this case, the customer buys a constant power level for a selected period.

The portal also checks how much the customer can buy.

The Purchasing Portal is only the customer interface.

NEPTUN remains the main system.

The portal sends the purchase to NEPTUN.

The purchasing data is stored only in NEPTUN.

10 · Další funkce

Další funkce portálu

⏱ cca 45 sekund

The customer can also see the history of previous purchases.

Another function is price monitoring.

In NEPTUN, we call this function Watchdog.

The customer can set a price limit for a selected market product.

When the price reaches this limit, the customer can receive a notification.

In some implementations, the customer can also update their future consumption plan.

11 · Závěr

Proč NEPTUN

⏱ cca 45 sekund

NEPTUN is not only a price calculator.

It connects all important data and processes in one place.

NEPTUN can also be integrated with other systems.

For example, it can be connected to CRM, billing, trading, SAP, or other systems.

The Purchasing Portal gives selected functions directly to the end customer.

For example, the customer can easily buy another tranche.

The main benefit is that we have the whole process in one system.

We do not need to manage it with several Excel files, emails, and manual notes.
